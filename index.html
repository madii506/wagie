How it works

Three rules. Same as any job, except this one pays in stock.

How payroll works

Your first job where the HR department is a smart contract.

Step 1 · The stock token

$ADP exists on-chain

Robinhood issues tokenized US stocks on Robinhood Chain. The $ADP token tracks the share price of Automatic Data Processing. It is not a share certificate and carries no voting rights.

Step 2 · The timesheet

clockIn() once per day

The WagieClock contract records the day you clocked in and counts your attendance for the pay period. It cannot be faked — the timestamp is the chain's.

Step 3 · Strikes

Three misses, you're fired

Miss a day: written warning. Miss three in one pay period: terminated — no payout that period, attendance resets. Clock in the next day and you're rehired at the bottom.

Step 4 · Payday

Fees → $ADP → you

Every trade on the pair pays a fee. On payday the pool is bought as $ADP and split among wallets with attendance: your share = your WAGIE balance × your days worked, over the total.

Plain-English version, not a prospectus. Tokenized stocks are not the same as owning shares, prices can go to zero, and WAGIE is a memecoin. Payroll is only as big as the fees the pair generates; some Fridays it will be small.

Insane ADP lore

The company that pays America, in order. Every line links to its source.

1949

Started above a luncheonette

Henry Taub and his brother Joe founded "Automatic Payrolls, Inc." in Paterson, New Jersey, doing payroll by hand from an office above a lunch counter. source

1954

A future US senator was a partner

Frank Lautenberg joined early and was a full partner by 1954. He went on to serve five terms in the United States Senate. source

1957 – 1961

Punch cards, then Wall Street

In 1957 the calculators were replaced by a punched-card system. The company renamed itself Automatic Data Processing in 1958 and went public in 1961 with 300 clients, 125 employees and about $400,000 in revenue. source

1985 →

One in five paychecks

From the mid-eighties on, ADP has processed the paychecks of roughly 20% of the US workforce. Today it's about 67,000 employees and $20.6B in revenue. source

Every month

The jobs number the market front-runs

The ADP National Employment Report, built from 26 million+ workers' data, comes out two days before the government's jobs report — and in 2025 ADP stopped sharing its employment data with the Federal Reserve. source

FAQ

Short answers. HR is not available for further questions.

What does "paired to $ADP" mean?

The liquidity pool is WAGIE against tokenized Automatic Data Processing stock on Robinhood Chain. Fees on the pair are paid out in $ADP, not in WAGIE.

Do I really have to clock in every day?

Yes. One transaction to the punch-clock contract per day. The contract records the day; the payday script only pays wallets with attendance in that period. Weekends count — this job has no weekends.

What happens if I get fired?

Three missed days in one pay period means no payout for that period and your attendance resets. Clock in the next day and you're rehired. Nobody takes your WAGIE — they just stop paying you.

Is this affiliated with ADP?

No. WAGIE is a community memecoin. Automatic Data Processing did not make it, endorse it, or hear about it. The lore above is public record.

Where is the contract?

There isn't one yet. The token, the punch clock and the pool appear in the Token section the moment they deploy, and are posted as the first reply to the launch tweet.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a job that pays in a memecoin's fees. Only spend what you can afford to lose, and clock out on time.
